# Why does it matter?

## Is short sleep connected to students' mental health?

Most students don't get enough sleep. Here we compare students' average mental health score (0–10) across four groups based on how many hours they sleep per night.

<div class="tip">
  <strong>Key finding:</strong> students who sleep less than 6 hours have the lowest average mental health score (about 5.1 out of 10). Students who sleep 8 hours or more score about 7.6.
