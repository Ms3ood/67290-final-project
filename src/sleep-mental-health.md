# Why does it matter?

## How is short sleep connected to mental health and grades?

<div class="tip" style="max-width: none;">
<strong>Key findings:</strong> students who sleep less than 6 hours have the lowest average mental health score (about 5.1 out of 10, vs 7.6 for 8+ hours). High schoolers with mostly D's/F's are the least likely to get 8+ hours of sleep (14%, vs 25% of students with mostly A's).
</div>
