# Is phone use connected?

## Do students who spend more time on social media sleep less?

<div class="tip" style="max-width: none;">
<strong>Key findings:</strong> teens who check social media about once an hour or more are the least likely to get enough sleep: only 20% sleep 8+ hours on a school night, compared with 37% of teens who don't use social media, and about half (51%) sleep 6 hours or less. The student-level data shows the same pattern: almost every student using social media 6+ hours a day sleeps under 7 hours.
</div>
