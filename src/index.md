# Phones vs. Sleep

Most students don't get enough sleep, and heavy phone use often comes with it. This dashboard tells that story in three parts:

1. **[How big is the problem?](./sleep-trends)** How many teens get enough sleep, and how that has changed over the past decade.
2. **[Is phone use connected?](./screens-and-sleep)** How time on social media relates to how much students sleep.
3. **[Why does it matter?](./sleep-mental-health)** How short sleep connects to students' mental health.

<div class="note">
Our data shows correlation, not causation. One of our datasets is partly simulated and is used only to illustrate the relationship.
</div>
